Questions, answered

Is GST included in the price shown?⌄
No — prices are exclusive of GST. 18% GST applies on the subscription, so a ₹2,499/month plan is ₹2,949 all-in. As a chartered accountancy firm, we issue a proper tax invoice you can claim input credit against.
What happens after the 14-day trial?⌄
Your account stays active with full features during the trial. At the end, pick a plan to continue. Your data is never deleted without notice, and nothing is charged automatically unless you choose a paid plan.
Can I change plans later?⌄
Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply from your next billing cycle, as long as your usage fits the lower plan's limits.
Is my company's data separated from other companies?⌄
Completely. Every record is isolated at the database level, so one company can never see another's vendors, POs, or invoices — even through the API. This is enforced by row-level security, not just the interface.
Do vendors pay anything?⌄
No. Vendors sign in free to view the POs you send them and upload their invoices. The subscription is billed only to your business.
